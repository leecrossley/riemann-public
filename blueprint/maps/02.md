## Survey vs Lean

- Levels (survey def:level): the optional exceptional character `psK` is required by `KSet.psK_spec` to be in KEPT, **odd and real**. The survey omits "odd, real". Recorded in an:level.
- Survey rem:junk says the Landau branch supplies zeta(1) != 0 via `riemannZeta_one_ne_zero` at poleData.lean:196. In `Deduction.lemmaL` the case Re s >= 1 (which includes s = 1 for the character mod 1) is discharged by `charFamily_LFunction_ne_zero_of_one_le_re`, i.e. Mathlib's `riemannZeta_ne_zero_of_one_le_re` (whose statement covers s = 1 via the junk value). poleData.lean:196 uses `riemannZeta_one_ne_zero` only inside `poleData_char`, to show g(w) != 0 <-> zeta(w) != 0 at w = 1 for g(s) = (s-1)zeta(s) extended by g(1)=1.
- Survey sketch step (1) residue "-mu a/(rho(rho-1))": Lean residue is S*(-1/(rho(rho-1))) with S = sum_i (a_i/2)(m1_i + m2_i), m1, m2 the orders of L(chi_i) and L(chi_i^{-1}) at rho. Phi = sum (a_i/2)(Phi_{chi_i} + Phi_{chi_i^{-1}}), using Re z = (z + conj z)/2 and conj Gchi_chi = Gchi_{chi^{-1}}.
- Survey prop:realcomplex third claim (Sz(y) = y int_1^y Ch(t)/t^2 dt): no Lean counterpart found (Formalism.Sz is used only in StatementOC.lean's own definitions); not included as a node.
- Survey lists the trivial-character branch of g_chi only for "chi = 1"; in Lean `An.gChi` tests `χ = 1` at the given modulus, so a principal character of modulus > 1 also gets G(y). `CharFamily.adm` (N = 1 or chi != 1) prevents such characters from entering the Landau development; `level_family` is admissible because a primitive character equal to 1 has modulus 1.
- Survey's sketch for `landau_tails` is right in substance; explicit choices are l = 2 + 6/delta, sigma = l(1+delta)(2+l), k = ceil((1+delta/2) sigma U).
