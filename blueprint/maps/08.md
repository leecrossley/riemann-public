## Open questions

1. **Possible over-strength of `Row0b`.** `Row0b recT` quantifies over *every* map with
   `IsRecT`, and the weak laws pin a map only on good classes. Suppose some `IsRecT` map sent a
   non-good representation (a positive-depth cuspidal over `Q_g`, say) to a value that is tame,
   square-free and has `N=0`. Then clause `away.tame0` would assert an equality of inertial
   characteristic polynomials with a value that need not be the true parameter, and `Row0b`
   could be false. Injectivity on good targets and the type law may exclude this. That would
   need every tame irreducible parameter to be the image of a good cuspidal under every `IsRecT`
   map, which was not checked. This deserves a targeted read of `LawsT` against
   `AwayB.tame0`/`AboveB.nice`.
2. Whether Grothendieck's monodromy theorem is available, so that the default branch of `wdOf`
   (trivial diagonal) is never taken at `w ∤ l`. Not located.
3. `TopShape` and `TopShapeAlt` have the same argument names; their types were not compared.
