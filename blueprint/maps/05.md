## Survey vs Lean

- **HLat positivity.** The survey says "a positive hermitian Gram matrix at each infinite place".
  `HLat` (LRC/DataArith.lean:61) has an arbitrary complex matrix `X v` per infinite place. It is a
  *frame* (`fcoord` = `(X v)⁻¹ · σ_v(coords)`), and `Good` asks only for invertible determinants.
  It has no hermitian form and no positivity.
- **Slope normalisation.** `muDef = 2·slope_Z(realization) − log 2 + log|d_K|/[K:Q]`, and
  `htMap = (1/n_∞) Σ_v log ‖·‖_v²`, archimedean only. This differs from the survey's
  `\hat\mu`/`h` with finite-place norms.
- **DivData.** The survey's Definition (period lattice data) builds integrality and the height
  bound into the definition. In Lean these are separate predicates (`OneMapIntoCC`, `HBound`). The
  structure's only inequality field is `HH_ge : sFrame + μ_max(RR) ≤ HH`, and it also has the
  fields `etaF`, `Dall` and `delP ≤ 1`.
- **FrameLA1 is proved.** The survey (Remark rem:frameF) says the frame theorem is "conditioned on"
  `FrameLA1`. In fact `frame_linear_algebra_first : FrameLA1` exists
  (HarderNarasimhanProofs/FrameLinearAlgebraFirst.lean:77), and `frame_theorem_sharp` applies it.
- **The −log|I| term is gratuitous.** `TheoremF` is obtained from `TheoremFSharp` by subtracting
  `log #I ≥ 0` (`theoremF_of_sharp`). The record's `SlopeIneq` carries the same weakened term.
- **Free normalising matrices in TheoremF.** `Y i` and `Y'` are arbitrary invertible matrices and are
  not tied to the frames `X`. The survey's "normalised coordinates" suggests that they are tied.
- **Main-term proposition.** The survey's Prop (prop:mainterms) assumes `a_τ > 0` and `w > 0`. The
  Lean lemma `Deduction.ch_top_main_eq` (LT/ChTop.lean:12) needs no positivity, because `MAIN` is
  defined with `Real.log` directly. It is stated with `IdentU` (a shift map `tr`); `Ident` is the
  case `tr = id`. No Lean lemma was found under the survey's `gap` form.
- **ColumnChain is abstract.** `cls i`, `stab`, `Y i` and `unf` are free fields. The survey's reading
  "class set of the genus of a unimodular lattice in X_i, algebraic automorphic forms on U(X_i)" is
  not enforced by `ColumnChain`. Nothing in it references `Spaces`.
- **Spaces.** The survey lists Y_0 = X_0 ⊕ ⟨−T⟩ ⊕ hyperbolic planes. Lean gives only the vectors and
  their orthogonality relations, not a decomposition. The rank identity `rank_base_space` makes it
  one.
- **Ties are discarded downstream (important).** `ChainTied.core_of` (TieGlobal.lean:116) discards
  all four tie predicates. `UpperTheory.Comparison₂.tiedCore_of_chainTied`
  (PC/LevelGmLeOnFamilyScales.lean:13), which is on the path to `level_gm_le_on_family_scales` and
  `zeroFreeOn_of_chainTied`, discards `TiePts`, `TieGlobal` and `TieLocal`, and keeps only the
  `TT`/`T2` descriptions and the ℓ-values from `TieArith`. The lattice identifications are therefore
  never used after `ChainTied` is established. They matter only for *proving* `ChainTied`
  (e.g. LossesSmallShares.lean, FamRec.lean; not read).
