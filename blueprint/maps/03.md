## Survey versus Lean

- `HostRaw` has a field `two_le_e : 2 ≤ φ.e` that the survey's Definition "hosts" omits.
  The chapter states it.
- Survey: "The archive derives (HostDer) that the Artin map is surjective, that E is CM...; the
  derivation was not checked." The derivation is a theorem, `Formalism.HC.HostRaw.der`
  (InertiaGaloisSymmetry/OfRawToHostRaw.lean:9), built from `Der.hostCore` and lemmas in
  `Formalism.HC.Der`. `HostAx.ofRaw` uses it, so `HostAx` adds no hypotheses. The surjectivity,
  CM and degree parts were read in full (CycBasic.lean); the rest was not expanded.
- `HostDer` states much more than the survey lists: Frobenius, inertia and decomposition groups,
  CM type, different of E/K, discriminants, and root-discriminant bounds.
- `DerOfRaw` (ConjEMemHostFIff.lean:172) is stated and never used. `HostRaw.der` is the live proof.
- The survey's Proposition "psifieldchars" (the characters of Gal(E/Q) are the products
  ε ξ χ_q^i χ_d^j) has no Lean counterpart. The archive uses `TwistForm` instead, proved for the
  constructed host by `UpperTheory.Top.twistAny`. The chapter has no node for the survey's
  proposition.
- Survey's open question on the constant 4 in `|L'/L(1,χ)| ≤ 4 log y` (PairGood clause 1):
  `QH.pairEx` adds it by calling `QH.kappa4` with A = 3 (pairEx.lean:117, 161-164). It does not
  come from `box_of_S`. See remark `host:rem-four`.
- Survey `conj:fieldchi`: the Lean statement also returns `FieldRows H b`, `QS ∣ lcm(2^{e+2}, N_ψ)` and
  `QS ≤ 4 N_ψ ord`. These are in the chapter.
- `QH.stage2` applies `QH.stage` with exponent `6ε/5` rather than ε, with m0 = 16·oddM(z,q).
- The survey's `ZD.zero_density_box` lives in namespace
  `Formalism.HermitianLattices.Ext.PrimitiveCharacterCasts.ZD` (CountZAll.lean:13).

## Misleading or hollow names

- `HostAx`: suggests assumed axioms; all are proved (remark `host:rem-der`).
- `Formalism.HC.HostConditions`: looks like the analytic hypothesis package. In the archive it
  only appears as a hypothesis: of `EAtH.explicit_formula_error_of_host_conditions` and of a lemma
  in CoprimeRanks/OfPInjective.lean:130. Nothing on the `supplyChi` path builds it. (There is a
  second, unrelated `HostConditions` structure in QuadraticCharacters/A0InfStabilizerTrivial.lean:139.)
- `ExceptionalCharacterKept` for `Level.cyc ψ` is close to empty: psK = none, so it says only that
  ω_q is not kept (remark `host:rem-exc`).
- `HostEx` is `HostExW` with requirement `True`.
- `admissible_ranks_of_CMDatum` shows that `UpperTheory.Top.admitted` always holds. It is a
  hypothesis-shaped definition that is in fact always satisfied.
