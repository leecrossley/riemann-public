\caption{The admissible pairs of Definition~\ref{def:admissible} for
$q=121$, $g=2$, $M=M'=11$, $\sigma=\mathrm{id}$, with the generic
Riemann--Roch values $\ell(jP_0)=\max(1,j+1-g)$. Condition (N) is $a<11$;
the region where it fails is hatched. Condition (S) holds strictly above
the orange dash-dot curve $(a-g)(b+1-g)=Ma$ of \eqref{eq:siegel-margin}.
Orange triangles: admissible pairs; grey dots: the others. The level lines
of the bound $1+a+M'b$ have slope $-1/M'$, so the best pair takes $a$ as
large as (N) allows and then $b$ as small as (S) allows: $(10,15)$ (star),
with $N\le176$. It is both the pair of Proposition~\ref{prop:balancing} and
Bombieri's pair (Theorem~\ref{thm:bombieri}). Green: the level
$166=q+1+2g\sqrt q$ of the Weil bound, below every admissible pair.
Computed by enumerating the lattice points.}
