\caption{Proposition~\ref{prop:landau} in the $t$-plane. Each column shows
a sequence $a_k=\sum_jc_j\beta_j^k$, the poles $t=1/\beta_j$ of
$\sum_ka_kt^k=\sum_jc_j\beta_jt/(1-\beta_jt)$ (vermillion crosses, the
dominant ones larger), the disc of convergence $\lvert t\rvert<1/4$
(grey), and below it the values $a_k/4^k$ for $k=1,\dots,30$ (blue bar:
$a_k\ge0$; purple hatched bar: $a_k<0$). (a) $a_k=4^k+2\Real
\bigl((3e^{i\pi/3})^k\bigr)$ is nonnegative for every $k\ge1$, and the
pole on the boundary of the disc is on the positive real axis, at $1/4$,
with positive coefficient, as the proposition requires. (b)
$a_k=2\Real\bigl((4e^{i\pi/3})^k\bigr)+3^k$ has its poles on the boundary
of the disc at $e^{\pm i\pi/3}/4$, off the real axis (purple circles), and
$a_k<0$ whenever $k\equiv2,3,4\pmod6$, so on every window of three
consecutive $k$. The proposition excludes this configuration for a
sequence that is nonnegative on arbitrarily long intervals. Values from
the closed formulas.}
