\caption{Frobenius cosets (Proposition~\ref{prop:frob-coset}) in a Galois
extension $E/F$ of function fields with group $G=S_3=\{\mathrm{id},\rho,
\rho^2,\tau_1,\tau_2,\tau_3\}$ and full constant field $\F_q$, over a place
$Q$ of $F$ of degree one; such places occur, for example, in the Galois
closure of a cubic extension of $\F_q(x)$. Left in each panel: the
geometric points above $Q$, grouped into places (Frobenius orbits, arrows).
Right: $D(P)$ as cosets of $I(P)$ (outlined boxes), mapped onto
$\Gal(\kappa(P)/\F_q)$ by $\sigma\mapsto\bar\sigma$. (a) $Q$ unramified with
Frobenius a $3$-cycle: two places above $Q$, each of degree $3$, with
$D(P)=A_3$ and $I(P)=\{\mathrm{id}\}$; the coset over $\Fr_q$ is
$\Phi(P)=\{\rho\}$ (blue), and $\Phi(P')=\{\rho^2\}$. (b) $Q$ ramified
with $D(P_i)=I(P_i)=\{\mathrm{id},\tau_i\}$: three places of degree one,
and $\Phi(P_i)=I(P_i)$ (blue). In both cases
$\lvert\Phi(P)\rvert\deg P=\lvert D(P)\rvert$ and $\sum_{P\mid
Q}\lvert D(P)\rvert=\lvert G\rvert=6$. The bottom rows give, for each
$\sigma\in G$, the degree of the $\sigma$-Frobenius places above $Q$; each
row sums to $\lvert G\rvert$, so every place of degree one of $F$, ramified
or not, contributes exactly $\lvert G\rvert$ to $\sum_\sigma N(E,\sigma)$
(Proposition~\ref{prop:class-sum}). Blue: the $\sigma$ for which a place
above $Q$ is $\sigma$-Frobenius. Schematic positions; the group theory is
exact.}
\label{fig:frob-coset}
