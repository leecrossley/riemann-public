\caption{Schematic, not to scale: where the two kinds of weight test the
functions $\GPsi_\theta$ (Definition~\ref{def:thick}). Since
$|a_\tau/b-x|\le1$, a thick weight gives an argument $a_\tau/w$ near $x$,
which the proof moves to exactly $x$. A thin weight gives
$a_\tau/w\ge(x-1)b/(2b')\ge\frac{x-1}{x}\cdot\frac{X^\flat}{2}$, by
$b'X^\flat\le xb$, and the thin-scale bound is assumed from $X^\flat/3$
on (grey). At the sizes used later, $b=\exp(x^\eps)$ and
$X^\flat=\exp(x^{\eps/3})$, so the true distances are far larger than
drawn.}
