\caption{Schematic map of the clauses of Definition~\ref{def:size-data}
at one shift $u_0$ and one word $u$. Arrows show which quantities each
clause relates; they are not implications. Clause~\ref{item:sd-fs}
bounds the slopes of the form lattice, clause~\ref{item:sd-qs} passes
to the slopes of $N_u$, shifted by the gain $\Ga$, the field term
$\Fo$ and the congruence size $\Cg(u)$, and the frame slope $s$ and the
excess $u_F$ are formed from the slopes of $N_u$ and the heights $h(u)$
(Definition~\ref{def:record}). Clause~\ref{item:sd-slope} (blue)
bounds $\mu_R$ from below by $s$ minus the slack
$\log|\Words(u_0)|+(1-\beta_0)^{-1}\bigl(4u_F\log(1+\Delta_R/u_F)
+(\beta_1+4\sqrt{\beta_2})\Delta_R\bigr)$, and
clause~\ref{item:sd-div} (vermillion) bounds $\mu_R$ from above; there
$A(u)$ is the archimedean main term of Definition~\ref{def:upper-link}
and err the remaining terms of the clause. In
Chapter~\ref{ch:covolume} the second inequality of
clause~\ref{item:sd-qs} and clause~\ref{item:sd-div} give a lower bound
for $s$, clause~\ref{item:sd-slope} gives an upper bound, and $\mu_R$
and the heights cancel (Remark~\ref{rem:cancel} and
Figure~\ref{fig:sandwich}).}
