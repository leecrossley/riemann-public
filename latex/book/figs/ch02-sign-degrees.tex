\caption{Where the sign is: the cohomological degrees in the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:sign}. Blue cells hold the eigenvalues of $\Fr$ that occur:
the $\alpha_j$ on $H^1_c$ of the curve, their products $\alpha_I$ in degree
$k$ of the $k$-fold product, and the $\beta_j$ on the middle degree $k-1$ of
the quadric $Z$, which has dimension ${k-1}$. The white cells of the curve,
degrees $0$ and $2$, vanish by Hypothesis~\ref{H1}. The grey cells, below the
middle for $Z$, vanish because $0\notin U$ (Remark~\ref{rem:h2-half}). The dashed cells, the degrees $k,\dots,2k-2$ above
the middle, vanish only by Hypothesis~\ref{H2}, which is assumed for
infinitely many $k$, not proved, and fails for the plain path quadric at
$k=3$ in simple cases. The degrees $k$ and $k-1$ differ by one, the note's
``one off''; the minus sign in $R_n=q^nN_n-M_n^k$ then gives all
eigenvalues the sign $(-1)^{k-1}$ (Steps~3 and~4 of the proof), so
Proposition~\ref{lem:growth} applies. Schematic.}
