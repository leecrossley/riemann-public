\caption{The error of Proposition~\ref{prop:balancing}, divided by $\sqrt q$,
for $q=2^{20}$, $g=3$ and $M=2^1,\dots,2^{17}$, $M'=q/M$, on logarithmic
axes. Orange triangles: the total $(1+a+M'b-q)/\sqrt q$ of
\eqref{eq:balancing}. Grey dashed: its falling part $(g+1)M'/\sqrt q$, the
price of (N), which caps $a$ below $M'$. Grey dotted: its rising part
$M'\lfloor Mg/(M'-1-g)\rfloor/\sqrt q$, the price of (S); it is $0$ for
$M<\sqrt q$, where the total equals the falling part. Thin grey: the bound
$2gM/\sqrt q$ of the rising part from
Proposition~\ref{prop:balancing}. The minimum, $7$,
is at $M=\sqrt q$; by Proposition~\ref{prop:sqrtq-forced}, an error
$O(\sqrt q)$ requires $M\asymp_g\sqrt q$ when $g\ge2$. Green: Weil's constant
$2g=6$. Data: the closed formula
\eqref{eq:balancing}.}
