\caption{The $t$-plane for $q=3$ and the functional equation of
Theorem~\ref{thm:rationality}\eqref{item:rat-fe}. The map
$t\mapsto1/(qt)$ is inversion in the circle $\lvert t\rvert=q^{-1/2}$
(green), $t\mapsto1/(q\bar t)$, followed by complex conjugation. The zeros
of $L_K$ are closed under conjugation, because $L_K$ has real
coefficients, so they are also closed under inversion in that circle. The
map swaps the poles of $Z$ at $1/q$ and $1$ (vermillion crosses). Green
diamonds: the four zeros of $L=1+2t+6t^2+6t^3+9t^4$ for the genus-$2$
curve $y^2=x^5+x^2+1$ over $\F_3$, computed numerically from its point
counts. They lie on the circle,
as Theorem~\ref{thm:rh} proves in general. Purple open circles: a
hypothetical set of zeros $z$, $\bar z$, $1/(q\bar z)$, $1/(qz)$ off the
circle, which Theorem~\ref{thm:rationality} allows and
Theorem~\ref{thm:rh} excludes. Their position is schematic.}
\label{fig:zeta-plane}
