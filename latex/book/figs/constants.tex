\caption{The constants in front of $\sqrt q$, divided by $2g$, for
$1\le g\le10$. Orange, filled triangles: the constant $(g+1)\sqrt p+2g$ of
Proposition~\ref{prop:all-q}, valid for every $q=p^e$ with
$\sqrt q\ge2(g+1)$, for $p=7,5,3,2$
(solid, dashed, dash-dot, dotted); it tends to $1+\sqrt p/2$. Orange, open
triangles: $2g+\frac g{g+1}$ of Theorem~\ref{thm:bombieri} for $q$ a square
with $q>(g+1)^4$,
which tends to $1$. Green: Weil's sharp constant $2g$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:rh}). Grey dotted: $\max(2\sqrt g,2\sqrt{g(g-1)})$, which
by Proposition~\ref{prop:sqrtq-forced} the method of
Theorem~\ref{thm:aux-section} cannot reach when the Riemann--Roch values are
generic. It equals $2g$ at $g=1$ and lies below
it for $g\ge2$. Closed formulas.}
